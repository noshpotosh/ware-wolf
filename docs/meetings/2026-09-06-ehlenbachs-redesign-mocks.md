## Villain

Corporate-bland e-commerce template energy — hours notices, shipping
warnings, and login chrome burying a 58-year family cheese chalet
and Sissy the Cow.

## What we locked (design call)

- **Feel:** Cozy Wisconsin chalet — warm wood, dairy cream, aged
  cheddar gold, barn red sparingly. Not purple, not cream+terracotta
  AI-default, not dark mode, not SaaS blue.
- **Page set (3 max):** Homepage hero + story strip · Shop browse ·
  Visit us (Sissy). Product detail cut from this bet.
- **Hero rules:** Brand first, full-bleed place photo, one headline,
  one sentence, one CTA group. Operational noise out of the hero.
- **Cards:** Shop interactions only — never in the hero.
