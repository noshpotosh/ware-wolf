## What was said

PixelLab static art is reliable; motion sheets are finicky. Nosh
wants novel-game elements for storytelling/gameplay, Teams kept,
player-only movement, a stretched map with room flashes that reseat
NPCs, and a private founder office.

Fabrizio: reshape and ship a hybrid — not a full VN. Kill a second
chat that competes with Teams. Stations carry work state.

Maeve: loft = presence, VN = scene punctuation, Teams = long
continuity. Prove two rooms before a campus. Teleport-on-flash is
honest if reseats match work state.

## Calls locked by Nosh

1. **Rooms:** Founder’s Office + shared loft only (start with two).
2. **VN triggers:** proximity + `E`, **and** auto on blocker / ship.
3. **Desk OS:** founder office only.
4. **VN ↔ Teams:** fully separate in v1.
5. **Story:** flavor lines only — no first-week arc.
